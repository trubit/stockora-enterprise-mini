# Cashflow Intelligence & Multi-Horizon Forecasting

## Overview
Connects operating, investing, and financing cash movements to provide real-time liquid cash visibility and 30/60/90-day predictive forecasts.

### Multi-Horizon Forecast Bands
- **Horizon 30 Days**: High confidence ($92\%$) factoring in immediate sales velocity and due AR collections ($75\%$ collection rate).
- **Horizon 60 Days**: $84\%$ confidence interval ($p10/p50/p90$).
- **Horizon 90 Days**: Long-range liquidity runway projection ($p10/p90$ confidence envelope).
