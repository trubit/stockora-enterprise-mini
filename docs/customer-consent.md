# Customer Privacy, Consent Management & GDPR Compliance

## Overview
Ensures all customer engagement is governed by explicit consent records, channel preferences, and data privacy rights.

### Privacy Controls
1. **Channel Opt-In/Opt-Out**: Independent consent switches for Email, SMS, WhatsApp, Push, and In-App messaging.
2. **Audit Records**: Logs consent status, policy version (`v1.0`), capture timestamp, and IP/source channel.
3. **Data Portability & Export**: Provides 1-click JSON data export covering full profile, timeline, and purchase records.
4. **Controlled Account Merging**: Merges duplicate records with preserved historical audit references.
