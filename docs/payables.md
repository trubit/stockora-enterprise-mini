# Accounts Payable (AP) & Supplier Sourcing Obligations

## Overview
Accounts Payable manages obligations to suppliers generated by purchase orders, goods receipts, and 3-way matching.

### Workflows
1. **Goods Receipt**: Emits `purchase.recorded`, auto-posting $\text{Debit: Inventory (1300)}, \text{Credit: Accounts Payable (2000)}$.
2. **Disbursement**: Settling invoices posts $\text{Debit: Accounts Payable (2000)}, \text{Credit: Bank Account (1010)}$.
3. **Aging Reports**: Categorizes liabilities across standard 30-day buckets.
