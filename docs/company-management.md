# Company Profile & Master Settings

## 1. Company Profile Management

Company Owners can update their enterprise metadata via `/company/settings`:
- **Identity**: Legal entity name, registration number, business type, industry vertical.
- **Contact**: Administrative email, customer support phone, corporate website.
- **Physical & Billing Address**: Street, city, state/province, postal code, country.

## 2. Branding Customization

- **Brand Palette**:
  - `primaryColor` (Hex): Used on active buttons, highlights, badges, and top accents.
  - `secondaryColor` (Hex): Used for secondary callouts and borders.
  - `accentColor` (Hex): Used for status chips and operational alerts.
- **Assets**:
  - `logoUrl`: Displayed in the top navigation bar, invoice PDFs, and thermal receipt headers.
- **Receipt & Invoice Layout**:
  - `receiptHeader`: Custom greeting or legal notice printed on all POS receipts.
  - `receiptFooter`: Return policy, warranty terms, and QR code promotional notices.
