# Financial Reporting & Statements (P&L, Balance Sheet, Trial Balance)

## Overview
Generates standard financial statements derived from double-entry ledger accounts.

### Reports Generated
1. **Profit & Loss (P&L)**:
   - Gross Revenue $-$ Discounts/Returns = Net Revenue
   - Net Revenue $-$ COGS = Gross Profit
   - Gross Profit $-$ Total Operating Expenses = Operating Profit
   - Operating Profit $-$ Tax Expense = Net Income
2. **Balance Sheet**:
   - Current Assets + Non-Current Assets = Total Assets
   - Current Liabilities + Long-Term Debt = Total Liabilities
   - Contributed Capital + Cumulative Retained Earnings = Total Equity
   - Verifies: $\text{Assets} = \text{Liabilities} + \text{Equity}$
3. **Trial Balance**:
   - Lists all debit and credit balances per account and verifies zero-sum equality ($\sum \text{Debits} == \sum \text{Credits}$).
