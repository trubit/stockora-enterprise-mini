# Personalized Product Recommendation Engine & Explainability

## Overview
Generates individualized product recommendations based on verified historical purchase behavior, category affinity, and collaborative basket patterns.

### Recommendation Strategies
1. **Frequently Bought Together**: Analyzes co-occurrence in completed transaction line items.
2. **Category Affinity**: Matches customer's preferred categories and previous purchase frequency.
3. **Natural Language Rationale**: Every recommendation provides a transparent reason (e.g. *"Recommended because you previously purchased X"*, *"Popular in your preferred category"*).
