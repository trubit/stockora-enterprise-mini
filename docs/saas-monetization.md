# SaaS Monetization & Growth Architecture

## 1. Multi-Tenant Pricing Strategy
Stockora Enterprise monetizes across three core dimensions:
1. **Capacity Scaling**: Quotas for users, store branches, warehouses, POS checkout terminals, and catalog products.
2. **Feature Access**: Tiers unlocking advanced ERP modules (CRM, Loyalty, Copilot AI Assistant, REST APIs, Automation workflows).
3. **Consumption-Based Metering**: Monthly AI request tokens, external API call volume, and storage allocations.

---

## 2. Platform Administration & Revenue Metrics
Platform administrators have access to global SaaS analytics:
- **MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)**: Normalized monthly subscription revenue.
- **ARR (Annual Run Rate)**: Annualized recurring revenue projection.
- **Plan Distribution**: Breakdown of tenants on Free, Starter, Professional, Business, and Enterprise plans.
- **Trial Conversion**: Tracking pipeline of trialing companies.
