# AI Financial Assistant & What-If Decision Simulators

## Overview
Powered by `FinancialAIService` utilizing live database grounding and multi-provider LLM execution.

### Key Capabilities
1. **Evidence-Backed CFO Assistant**: Answers natural language inquiries regarding cash runway, profit variances, AR recovery priorities, and supplier terms with exact verified numbers.
2. **What-If Scenario Simulator**: Simulates the financial bottom-line impacts of sales shifts ($\pm 15\%$), price elasticity adjustments ($\pm 5\%$), operating expense inflation ($\pm 10\%$), and supplier COGS changes ($\pm 8\%$) without mutating live ledger data.
